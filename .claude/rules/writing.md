# Writing

Typographic characters that people rarely type read as AI tells. In code, comments, UI copy, docs and commit messages, use what a keyboard gives you:

- `x`, not `×`: "33 x 33 modules"
- `...`, not `…`, and don't trail off with either
- `->` or words, not `→`
- `-` or a rephrase, not `—` or `–`
- "about 124 bits" or the exact number, not `≈` or `~`
- straight quotes `"` `'`, not curly ones
